1. **What is it?**: An agentic enterprise platform that ingests massive tenders and RFP specs, extracts requirements, performs hybrid RAG against corporate records, and generates an audit-ready compliance matrix with automated risk detection.
2. **Who is it for?**: General counsel, procurement teams, tender bidding specialists, and corporate compliance officers.
3. **What sets it apart?**:
   - Decoupled multi-agent pipeline (TenderComplianceOrchestrator).
   - Triple-LLM failover (Gemini ↔ NVIDIA NIM ↔ xAI Grok) with heuristic safety nets.
   - Dual-database parity: SQLite local dev + PostgreSQL pgvector HNSW index on Neon.
   - Multilingual support across 6 languages (EN, DE, FR, ES, NL, HI).
   - Reviewer feedback loop that auto-tunes future requirement evaluations.
4. **Visual Hook**: Glowing legal seal and digital RFP parser isolating mandatory contractual clauses in real time.
5. **Real data shown**:
   - Extraction taxonomy: Eligibility, Technical, Financial, Legal, Operational.
   - Real compliance states: Full, Partial, Missing with confidence scores.
   - Full Copilot Suite: Copilot Chat, Contract Drafter, Citation Researcher.
6. **One-line share caption**: \'Transforming 200-page tenders into audit-ready, winning bids: Multi-agent compliance reasoning, hybrid pgvector RAG, and an enterprise legal copilot suite.\'
